Delete your profile and close your account

You control your profile. Please review what closing your account means.

What will happen

  • Your preferred name, course, student reference and notification address will be removed from your platform profile.
  • Your platform sessions will end and you will no longer be able to submit or reply to requests.
  • Queued notifications will be stopped. A message already being delivered cannot be recalled.
  • Your university-wide sign-in account will remain with the university's account service.

Support records and privacy review

Closing your account does not mark outstanding support requests as resolved. Case records that need a lawful retention review are handled separately. We retain a minimal closure record to prevent accidental reactivation and to apply your choice after a backup restore.
