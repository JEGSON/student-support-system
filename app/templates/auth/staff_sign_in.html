From request to reply

  1. Manager: open Staff workspace and choose a request for your team.
  2. Manager: choose Assign officer, select an eligible officer, add a reason and confirm.
  3. Assigned officer or team manager: open the case and choose Start assigned work.
  4. Assigned officer or team manager: choose Add message, write the response and confirm the conversation action.
  5. Ask a question: choose Request information. The request then awaits the student's reply.

The student sees the response in their request's Conversation section. Internal notes remain staff-only.

Managers see their team scope; officers see assigned requests. An empty queue may mean the request belongs to another team or has not been assigned to you.
